# Blossom Dry Cleaners Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Blossom Dry Cleaners is a Dubai-based laundry and dry-cleaning business providing residential and commercial garment care services. Its offerings include dry cleaning, wet cleaning, washing, pressing, specialist garment care and collection and delivery services.

The redesigned homepage targets individuals, families, working professionals, apartment and villa residents, hotels and commercial businesses. The design focuses on clearer service communication, transparent collection and delivery information, and a more straightforward customer booking journey.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Pickup, Cleaning and Delivery Timeframes
- Separated pickup availability, cleaning turnaround and delivery information into clearer sections.
- Improved the presentation of express and regular laundry services.
- Added a clearer direction for customers to confirm current service availability instead of relying on conflicting timeframe promises.

### 2. More Consistent Branch and Service-Area Information
- Improved the distinction between physical branch locations and neighbourhoods covered by collection and delivery.
- Structured location information to make it easier for customers to identify nearby branches and service coverage.
- Avoided presenting inconsistent branch counts or unverified coverage as confirmed information.

### 3. Better Organized Services and Booking Journey
- Grouped laundry and specialist garment care services into clearer categories.
- Structured the customer journey around three steps: selecting a service, arranging collection and receiving delivery.
- Improved the visibility of contact and collection calls to action to make booking easier.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Refined the homepage to create a professional appearance aligned with Blossom's laundry and garment-care services.
- **Layout:** Improved the organization of service categories, collection information, locations and promotional content.
- **User Experience:** Made service selection, collection arrangements and contact options easier to follow.
- **Consistency:** Improved the presentation of service timeframes, branch information and delivery coverage.
- **Overall Appearance:** Created a more structured homepage that communicates Blossom's residential and commercial laundry solutions more clearly.

## 4. Project Summary

The Blossom Dry Cleaners homepage redesign addresses three key issues: conflicting service timeframes, inconsistent branch and service-area information, and a fragmented booking experience. The updated structure helps customers understand available services, identify relevant locations and follow a clearer process for arranging laundry collection and delivery.
