Helpful information

Before you arrange a collection.

Here are a few useful points to help you choose a service and confirm the details with the team.

What services does Blossom offer?

Blossom lists dry cleaning, laundry, wet cleaning, pressing, bedding and linen care, corporate laundry and selected specialist services. Contact the team to confirm current availability for a particular item.

Does Blossom offer pickup and delivery?

Blossom advertises collection and delivery. Availability may depend on your location and service requirements, so confirm the details with the team before booking.

How long does dry cleaning take?

Turnaround can vary by garment, service and order requirements. Ask the team to confirm the expected cleaning time and delivery window for your order.

Can I request express cleaning?

Express options are advertised for selected services. Confirm eligibility, timing and any applicable conditions with Blossom before arranging your order.

Does Blossom provide business laundry services?

Blossom lists corporate laundry services. Businesses can contact the team to discuss their items, volumes, schedule and service requirements.

How can I find my nearest branch?

Review the listed branch locations above or contact Blossom with your neighbourhood. The team can help confirm the nearest location and any collection coverage.
